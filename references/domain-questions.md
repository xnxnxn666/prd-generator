# Domain-Specific Question Templates

Pick the best-matching template based on the user's project description. Use the questions as a starting point — adapt to the specific context. Don't ask all of them mechanically.

## E-commerce / Marketplace

- What is being sold? Physical goods, digital products, services, or subscriptions?
- Who are the sellers? The platform owner only, or third-party merchants too?
- Payment flow: payment at checkout, escrow, invoice-based?
- Inventory management needed? Real-time stock tracking?
- Shipping / fulfillment: handled by seller, platform, or third-party logistics?
- Returns and refunds: what's the policy? Who handles disputes?

## SaaS / B2B Platform

- Subscription model? What tiers (free, pro, enterprise)?
- Team/workspace concept? Can multiple users belong to one organization?
- Onboarding flow: self-serve signup, demo-request, or sales-led?
- Is there an admin dashboard for customer organizations?
- Usage-based billing or flat subscription?
- API access for customers? Rate limiting?

## Social / Community

- Content type: text posts, images, video, links, polls?
- Relationship model: followers (asymmetric), friends (symmetric), or public-only?
- Feed algorithm: chronological, algorithmic ranking, or curated?
- Moderation: pre-moderation, post-moderation, user reporting, automated filters?
- Privacy model: public, followers-only, private, or groups?

## Admin Dashboard / Internal Tool

- What data is being managed? Who manages it?
- CRUD operations on what entities?
- Does it need to show analytics/statistics?
- Role-based access: who can see what?
- Is this replacing a spreadsheet or manual process?

## Data Dashboard / Analytics

- Data source: database, API, file upload, streaming?
- Real-time or batch? Refresh interval?
- Pre-built metrics or user-defined queries?
- Export formats needed?
- Who's the audience — executives, analysts, operations?

## Booking / Scheduling

- Who sets availability? Service providers or the system?
- Recurring bookings or one-time?
- Payment at booking time or after service?
- Cancellation/rescheduling policy?
- Calendar integration needed? (Google Calendar, Outlook)

## Content Management (CMS)

- Content types: articles, pages, media, custom blocks?
- Workflow: draft → review → publish? Who approves?
- Multi-language support?
- Scheduled publishing?
- Version history / rollback?
